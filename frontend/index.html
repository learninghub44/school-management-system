Core Modules

Everything your school needs, built in

From admissions to annual reports — CBC ERP covers the full lifecycle of school administration so your staff can focus on teaching.

📚

CBC Competency Tracking

Record and report learner competencies per CBC strand and sub-strand. Generate MoE-compliant progress reports with a single click.

👨‍🎓

Student Information System

Full learner profiles with enrollment history, guardian contacts, medical notes, and document uploads — all searchable instantly.

✅

Attendance Management

Daily attendance captured by class teachers, with automated alerts for absenteeism patterns and monthly summary reports.

💰

Fee Collection & Finance

Track school fees, generate receipts, manage arrears and bursaries. Integrates with M-Pesa for digital payments.

Teacher profiles, timetable assignments, leave management, and payroll summaries all in one place.

📊

Analytics & Reports

Visual dashboards on enrolment trends, performance analytics, and school-wide CBC progress with export to PDF and Excel.

📅

Timetable Generator

📱

Parent Communication

Send SMS and in-app notifications to parents for results, events, fee reminders, and school announcements.

Multi-School Administration

Super Admins can manage multiple schools from a single dashboard — ideal for school owners, NGOs, and county education officers.
